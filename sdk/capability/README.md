# Capability SDK

`Catalog` serves capability contracts (`BaseCatalog`); `VersionPolicy` decides contract compatibility. `BaseInvoker` runs the governed pipeline around an abstract `binding.Executor`: version, input schema (`contract.inputSchema`, an information shape resolved through `Definitions` or an embedded JSON Schema, checked before authority), actor lifecycle, operating limits (`Invocation.OperatingLimits`, the runtime's evaluations, any exceeded one denying), authority, approval, separation of duties (`SodChecker`, less the constraint a named `Invocation.SodExceptionID` relieves), information governance (`Invocation.InformationUses` against `information.Evaluator`, decisions evidenced), binding feature support, idempotency (`Ledger`, whose fenced claim reaches the caller as `Result.LedgerFence` for reconciling an unknown outcome), predicate preconditions (decided for a new attempt by a `predicate.Evaluator` on facts the `binding.Observer` reads; violated or unknown releases the claim and denies), execution, declared-outcome, business-error, and output-schema verification, effect verification (a `binding.Observer` evaluates the contract's postconditions after an accepted mutation, a postcondition predicate deciding from the observed facts; executed needs every required one satisfied on observed-fact evidence, a violation is failed or its mapped business error, a mix of held and violated is a partial effect that fails, escalates, and is never retried, and anything else is unknown with its cause recorded), and an `ActionRecord` appended to the evidence sink for every decision, carrying the assignment basis, operating-limit evaluations, and SoD exception the invocation presented. `Invocation.AuthorityChain` carries a downstream-verified acting path into authority and approval checks; any hop's approval bound requires approval, and SoD includes the performed-action history of the actor and every delegator. An `Escalator` (`BaseEscalator` over `BaseAccountableRecipient`) turns unknown and failed outcomes into an `ExceptionRecord` and an `Escalation` to the accountable authority of the agent's definition. `Reconciler` (`BaseInvoker.Reconcile`) settles an unknown attempt from observation alone: it proves the attempt's fence, or without one reclaims the unknown key through `Ledger.ReclaimUnknown` so a later sweep can settle it, never calls the transport, and appends a record naming the attempt it reconciles; an effect observed absent releases the key only when the contract sets `idempotency.retryWhenEffectAbsent`. `BaseInvoker.Readiness` reports, before a binding goes live, each denial that follows from documents and composition rather than from an action (contract, binding and declared features, input and output schemas and the data bindings of their shapes, an authority binding for the profile, and the approval gate, SoD checker, ledger, observer, predicate evaluator, and escalator the contract requires) as `Blocker` values naming the requirement. Tool and system adapters implement the transport and the observer; they never replace the contract.

`ledgertest.Run` is the contract suite a `Ledger` implementation runs from its own tests.
