# Authority SDK

`Evaluator` and `GrantSource` interfaces, `AbstractEvaluator` (enterprise, actor, capability, resource, validity, organizational context, certification, typed-limit, and presented delegation-bound comparison; embed it and supply a `GrantSource`), whose `Explain` returns the `Explanation` of the same evaluation (basis, unmet requirements, and `Redact` for viewers who may not see a source), `BaseGrantSource` (in-memory) and `DocumentGrantSource` (any `corpus.Source`), delegation-depth and separation-of-duties helpers. `CertificationLapse` suspends a grant whose certification is overdue or self-made, `Excuse` applies a `SodException` and fails closed when it does not relieve this actor now, and `LimitEvaluator.Widens` detects an operating limit looser than the one above it. `Request.AuthorityChain` carries a downstream-verified acting path; `DelegationPolicy` checks its shape, non-widening depth, validity, limits, and approval bound. `ApprovalGate` and `ApprovalSource` interfaces with `AbstractApprovalGate` (approved action, material-inputs digest, subjects, limits, and validity mode; embed it and supply an `ApprovalSource`), `BaseApprovalSource`, and `DocumentApprovalSource`. Evaluation is fail-closed and returns namespaced references, a result, and a reason.
