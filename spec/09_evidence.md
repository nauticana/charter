# Evidence, Audit, Exceptions, and Escalation

Evidence demonstrates what context, authority, decisions, and actions produced an outcome. An audit trail organizes evidence without requiring one storage technology.

## Requirements

- **CHR-EVID-001:** Governed action evidence MUST identify the actor, runtime context, responsibility or assignment, capability, action time, and outcome.
- **CHR-EVID-002:** Evidence for an executed action MUST identify the authority and approvals evaluated for that action.
- **CHR-EVID-003:** Evidence MUST distinguish observed facts, agent-produced assertions, human decisions, external-system responses, and derived conclusions.
- **CHR-EVID-004:** Corrections MUST append or link a superseding record and MUST NOT silently replace historical evidence.
- **CHR-EVID-005:** Evidence integrity and provenance MUST be verifiable to the degree required by the applicable conformance profile.
- **CHR-EVID-006:** An exception MUST identify the violated expectation, affected context, disposition state, and responsible escalation target.
- **CHR-EVID-007:** An escalation MUST identify its reason, recipient, requested decision, urgency, and resulting disposition when known.
- **CHR-EVID-008:** Evidence retention and access MUST follow the information-governance constraints attached to the underlying data and action.
- **CHR-EVID-009:** An implementation or conformance profile MUST NOT require disclosure of sensitive reasoning details or model traces when a sufficient decision rationale and evidence record can be produced without them.
- **CHR-EVID-010:** An attempt at a governed action that is denied, that fails, or whose external outcome is unknown MUST be evidenced with the same attribution as an executed action, MUST state that disposition explicitly rather than only in free text, and MUST record the authority, approval, and information-governance evaluations made before it stopped.
- **CHR-EVID-011:** The evaluation of a postcondition MUST state one of satisfied, violated, or unknown, the time of observation, and a reason, and a satisfied or violated evaluation MUST reference the observed-fact evidence supporting it. The external system's response and the independently observed state MUST remain distinct evidence records; a provider's acknowledgement is not evidence of the effect.
- **CHR-EVID-012:** An unknown outcome of a mutating action MAY be reconciled only through a new observation of its declared postconditions. When the observed effects establish a declared outcome, a new action record with executed disposition MUST be appended that identifies the attempt it reconciles, and the operation MUST NOT be submitted again. While the observation is unavailable or contradictory the outcome remains unknown and escalation continues to apply. Reconciliation MUST preserve the original attempt, its external response, and its uncertainty; it never restates the first record as success.
- **CHR-EVID-013:** An evaluation that justified releasing an agent definition MUST be evidenced with the definition and digest evaluated, the case set and version, the scores, and the judge and its version. Feedback on an outcome MUST be evidenced with the action rated, who rated it, the rating, and the reason. Either MUST be recorded as a human decision when the judge is a human and as an agent assertion or derived conclusion otherwise.
- **CHR-EVID-014:** An evidence record MAY state a fact: the subject, the information and attribute it concerns, the value, the time the value held in its source, and the system profile that stated it. A fact superseded by a later record, or whose time is more than the information's declared maximum age before a decision, MUST NOT count as current for that decision.
- **CHR-EVID-015:** An information definition MAY rank the sources of its facts, system profiles or evidence categories, most authoritative first; unlisted sources rank last. Among current facts about the same subject and attribute, a lower-ranked fact MUST NOT override a higher-ranked one, and facts at the highest present rank that disagree are a blocking conflict that MUST be surfaced and MUST NOT be resolved by recency alone. An executed action MUST NOT rely on a fact that is not current at its action time.
- **CHR-EVID-016:** The record of a mutating action whose required postconditions were observed MUST state its effect: complete when all hold, partial when some hold and others are violated, absent when none holds, and unobserved otherwise, and an unobserved effect MUST state why: the observation failed, a result was unevidenced, or observations contradicted each other. A partial effect is a failed action, whatever business error its violated postconditions map to; it MUST raise an exception record and MUST NOT be retried under its idempotency key, and a declared safe retry applies only to an absent effect.

Evidence formats may be centralized, distributed, signed, or externally referenced, provided their declared conformance requirements are met.
